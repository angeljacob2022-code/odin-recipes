Ogbono Soup

Everyone in Nigeria knows and loves a hot, delicious bowl of Ogbono soup! Renownd for its signature “draw” texture, this iconic Nigerian delicacy is not only visually appetizing but packed with incredible, rich and nutritious flavors that keeps you coming back for more. Made from ground wild mango seeds known as ogbono. It pairs effortlessly with your favorite swallow and even rice, making it a timeless stape in households across the country.

Ingredients for Ogbono Soup

  • Ground Ogbono seeds
  • Palm oil
  • Assorted meat like beef, goat meat, or shaki
  • Dried fish and stockfish
  • Ground crayfish
  • Seasoning cubes and salt to taste
  • Ground yellow or red pepper
  • Chopped leafy greens, ugwu, uziza, or bitter leaf
  • Onions
  • Water or meat stock

Steps to Prepare

  1. Place your meat in a pot and season with salt, onions, and seasoning cubes to taste. Cover and let it steam on low-medium heat for 10 to 15 minutes to absorb the flavors.
  2. Add water to the pot and continue cooking until the meat becomes soft and tender.
  3. Add fresh or dry pepper and crayfish, then let it simmer for another 5 to 7 minutes.
  4. In a small bowl, mix your ground Ogbono seeds with palm oil to form a soomth paste.
  5. Gently stir the Ogbono paste and then add it into the pot. Stir continuously as it dissolves and begins to thicken into its signature draw texture.
  6. Add more water if necessary
  7. Allow the soup to simmer on low medium heat so the Ogbono cooks through completely.
  8. Taste for seasoning and adjust salt or pepper if needed.
  9. Stir in your chopped uziza leaves (or any leafy green of your choice) and let it simmer for 5 to 10 minutes on low-medium heat.
  10. Turn off the heat. Your hot Ogbono soup is ready to serve with your favorite swallow!
